FAQ

Questions? We have answers.

Fresh Kerala fruits are flash-frozen to below -40°C. In a deep vacuum sublimation chamber, ice crystals vaporize directly into water vapor without ever melting into liquid. This retains 98% of natural vitamins, enzymes, and antioxidants while creating a delicate, airy crunch.
Yes, strictly 100% fruit. Every pack contains only ONE ingredient: the fruit named on the front (Mango, Chikku, Jackfruit, or Banana). No palm oil, no sunflower oil, no cane sugar, no sulphur dioxide, and zero artificial flavors.
All our produce is procured directly from certified family farms across Kerala—from the legendary sweet mango orchards of Palakkad to Varikka jackfruit groves in the Western Ghats. We are proudly "From God's Own Country".
Because our freeze-dried chips are naturally moisture-free, they love staying dry. Seal the zip-lock firmly after every snack session or keep them in an airtight container to maintain maximum crunch!
