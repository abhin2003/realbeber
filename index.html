The Science

Sublimation. Ice to vapor.

Unlike high-heat frying or dehydrating that destroys vitamins and creates trans fats, freeze-drying preserves 98% of natural cellular nutrition.

01

Tree-Ripened Harvest

Handpicked at peak sugar maturity from sustainable organic orchards across Kerala.

02

-40°C Cryo Freeze

Rapid sub-zero flash freezing immobilizes the fruit's water into microscopic ice crystals.

03

Vacuum Sublimation

Under deep vacuum chambers, ice converts directly into vapor without ever melting into water.

04

Barrier Nitrogen Seal

Flushed with food-grade nitrogen inside multi-layer light-blocking pouches for 12 months crispness.
